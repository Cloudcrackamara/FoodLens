This is FoodLens

FoodLens checks the registration number on a food pack against a register of product records. Point your camera at the NAFDAC or SON number and it tells you, in plain words, whether that number matches an active record, has expired, or belongs to a different product.

The register here is simulated: fictional demo records shaped like NAFDAC and SON data. A matching number shows what that register says. It is not a safety test and not a regulator’s decision.
