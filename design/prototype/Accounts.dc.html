FoodLens business

A workspace for the companies behind the packs.

  • Manufacturers list products, batches and depots that shoppers can check, and warn them about copies.
  • Wholesalers find suppliers whose products show an active registration and request stock.
  • Order requests only. No prices or payments go through FoodLens.

FoodLens reviews every company before it can publish. A reviewed profile is a FoodLens demo review, not NAFDAC or SON approval.
