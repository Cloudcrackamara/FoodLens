# Camera scanning accuracy sheet

Use real food packs (any brand). Do not photograph or store anything: just note what the confirm screen showed. Only record the printed text the scanner was asked to read.

How to run each pack:
1. On the phone, open `/check` through the tunnel (README) and tap **Scan pack**.
2. Scan the registration number, then the batch number.
3. On the confirm screen, compare with the pack and fill in one row.

Columns: **Reg OK** / **Batch OK** = the first reading was exactly right (Y), right after one tap on another reading (T), or had to be typed or corrected (N). Light: good / dim / glare. Print: dark-on-light, light-on-dark, embossed, curved.
